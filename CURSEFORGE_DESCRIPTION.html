Food Unification

Have you ever installed a bunch of cooking and farming mods and ended up with three different types of onion? Yeah, that's pretty much what this mod is here to fix.

Food Unification brings recipes together across different farming, cooking and food mods, so they work nicely with each other. The goal is to cut down on duplicate ingredients and make recipes connect across the mods in your pack.

Just install it alongside your favourite food mods and let it handle the compatibility!
